Engineered Process

How RAC Crew Works

Our structured 5-step engineering methodology ensures every AC unit achieves maximum cooling output and longevity.

01

Inspection

Comprehensive physical and electrical evaluation of indoor and outdoor units, refrigerant lines, and controls.

02

Diagnosis

Systematic fault isolation using digital multimeters, pressure gauges, and thermal diagnostic imaging.

03

Service

Execution of repairs, chemical jet wash, component replacement, or gas charging using genuine parts.

04

Testing

Operational stress test measuring airflow temperature differential, current draw, and gas pressure stability.

05

Follow-up

Quality assurance verification and ongoing maintenance recommendations from lead engineer Kashif Khan Fayyaz.
